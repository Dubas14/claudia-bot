# Suno packs (verbatim, scrubbed)

Two complete packs that produced the songs of the reference videos, as they were pasted into Suno v6's custom mode. Use
them as patterns, not as text to reuse: write the user's own style and lyrics with the same structure. The method behind
them is in `10-song.md`. The films' songs, lyrics and stems are never re-released or reused (claudia.gallery's norms):
make your own song.

---

## 1. *Escape Velocity* (EV) — 5:06, chosen by ear from several takes

**Why it worked:** concrete sound sources instead of genre adjectives, vocal gender and delivery stated per voice, the
negatives in the Exclude field, section tags that flip delivery (spoken deadpan verses so every line can carry a
reference; a sung euphoric chorus), numbers and acronyms spelled as sung, and a call-and-response hook that the final
chorus turns.

### Style

```
fashion show electroclash techno, 128 BPM, 4/4, dry analog kick, rolling acid bassline, cold detuned synth stabs, sweeping orchestral strings on the builds, deadpan female spoken-word verses, close and dry, clipped and confident, every word crisp and intelligible, euphoric sung female trance chorus with a supersaw lift and big clear vowels, crowd call-and-response shouts, stacked chanted vocals in the drop, a key change up into the last chorus, catwalk energy, cold, luxurious, optimistic, Berlin minimal austerity, clean modern mix
```

### Exclude styles

```
rap, rock guitar, lo-fi, male lead vocal, mumbled vocals, big room EDM, dubstep
```

## 2. *Clodyssey (Superpersuasion)* (SP) — 3:10, the 7th take of draft 9

**Why it worked:** the sequel kept the singer (a Suno Voice made from the first song) and the genre, but changed the
device (no counting: a dialogue chorus that flips from TIE ME TO THE MAST! to UNTIE ME!), removed the strings the user
disliked, used section cues as arrangement controls ("the bass drops out"), and was tuned line by line by the user's ear
with a scansion tool calibrated on their verdicts.

### Settings (every take)

- Model v6, custom mode; **Voice** attached ("EV Combined", one combined spoken + sung Voice made from the first song;
  Suno allows one Voice per source song); Voice mode "Voice (new)"; when Suno asks "Overwrite Styles?" answer **Keep
  Current**.
- **Audio Influence 75 %** (default 25 %) · **Variety off** ("Exact style") · **Max Mode on** · Duration Auto · Weirdness
  50 % · Style Influence 50 % · My Taste off.

### Style

```
Electroclash techno, clean modern mix with close, dry vocals; deadpan, clipped female spoken verses with intimate whispered asides rise into euphoric sung choruses answered by stacked crowd shouts; dry analog kick, rolling acid bassline, cold detuned synth stabs, a rising siren synth on the builds, hardgroove percussion in the break, supersaw chorus lift; 128 BPM hypnotic 4/4 pulse, key change up into the final chorus
```

### Exclude styles

```
rap, rock guitar, lo-fi, male lead vocal, mumbled vocals, big room EDM, dubstep, strings, orchestral strings
```

### Section cues (the arrangement controls, verbatim)

```
[Intro - spoken, close, no beat, then one sung line a cappella]
[Verse 1 - spoken, deadpan, second person, the beat kicks in]
[Chorus - sung, trance lift, answered by crowd shouts]
[Verse 2 - spoken, deadpan, bouncy]
[Verse 3 - spoken, deadpan, darker, bouncy, the bass drops out]
[Break - spoken over the kick, then chanted, stacked voices]
[Final Chorus - sung, key change up, euphoric]
[End]
```

"darker, the bass drops out" made Verse 3 punchier. A long cue ("bouncy and punchy, the beat stops and starts, big kick
slams on the line ends") was tested against the single word "bouncy"; the final take keeps the plain word.

**Notes on the details that mattered:**
- **The Break** ("It went down. It was that good.") was the user's model of good scansion: two short clauses, a stop,
  ending on a stress. Earlier drafts were rebuilt on its shape.
- **Every couplet rhymes on the beat-4 word** (toe/know, all/stall, psychosis/hypnosis, could/good). Spoken lines are
  10–13 syllables so Suno doesn't drag them.
- **Respell only in the Suno field** ("Dahrio" was sung correctly; the on-screen lyric keeps "Dario").
- **One word mattered:** "for three days and she won" (no full stop) stopped Suno rushing the line.
- Hooks travel, verse jokes don't: one singable hook with call and response, one arguable claim, one quotable line.
- After v1 was approved, the user re-sang "Superpersuasion" in Suno's editor: export with **BPM lock off** and time-map the
  approved video to it (`90-master-publish.md` §3.1).
